## Task 1: Sentence Tokenization & Length-Stratified Splits

### 1. Sentence Tokenization
IndicCorp v2 rows contain full multi-sentence paragraphs. We sentence-tokenize raw paragraphs using Indic sentence boundary delimiters:
- Purna Viram `।` (U+0964)
- Double Purna Viram `॥` (U+0965)
- Standard delimiters `.`, `?`, `!`
- Urdu Dahi `۔` (U+06D4)

Sentences are stripped of extraneous whitespace and filtered to remove noisy fragments ($< 3$ words or $< 12$ characters) or corrupt strings.

### 2. Length-Distribution Stratified Split Creation
To ensure the **Development (1,000 sentences)** and **Test (1,000 sentences)** sets do not consist of similar sentence lengths and accurately reflect the corpus length variance:
1. Sentence character lengths are computed across the extracted dataset pool.
2. Quantile binning (10 decile bins) partitions the corpus into 10 distinct length strata ranging from short to very long.
3. Stratified random sampling selects proportional sentence counts from every decile bin to form the Dev (1,000) and Test (1,000) splits.
4. The remaining $> 1,000,000$ sentences constitute the training pool, subsampled into 100k, 300k, 500k, and 1M subsets.

## Task 2: Subword Tokenizer Implementations

### 1. Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE)
- **Word Dictionary**: Represents each word $w$ with corpus frequency $f(w)$ as a space-separated sequence of characters terminated by `</w>`.
- **Base Vocab**: Individual characters + special tokens (`<unk>`, `<pad>`, `<bos>`, `<eos>`).
- **Merge Criteria**: Iteratively merges the adjacent subword pair $(A, B)$ with maximum occurrence frequency across the corpus.
- **Inference (Encoding)**: Applies learned merge rules ordered by training rank to split unseen test sentences into subwords.

### 2. WordPiece
- **Word Dictionary**: Represents each word with initial character $c_1$ and continuation characters prefixed with `##` ($c_2, c_3, \dots$).
- **Base Vocab**: Characters + `##` continuation subwords + special tokens (`[UNK]`, `[PAD]`, `[CLS]`, `[SEP]`, `[MASK]`).
- **Likelihood Scoring Formula**:
  $$\text{Score}(A, B) = \frac{\text{Count}(A, B)}{\text{Count}(A) \times \text{Count}(B)}$$
- **Merge Criteria**: Merges the pair $(A, B)$ maximizing the likelihood score, favoring subwords that appear together significantly more often than expected by chance.
- **Inference (Encoding)**: Uses **MaxMatch** (greedy longest-match prefix matching) to segment words into vocabulary tokens.
